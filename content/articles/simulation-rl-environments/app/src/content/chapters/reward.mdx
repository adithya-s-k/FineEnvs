## How the reward works

**Deterministic**: the same plan gets the same score every time. No LLM judge, no sampling; one grade per episode,
on `submit_plan`.

It is hard to game: an independent checker re-verifies every plan, and the naive re-plan and a greedy heuristic stay
below 0.6 and 0.85 because tasks they nearly solve are dropped when the dataset is built.

**The example in the film.** The recorded GLM-5.3 run checks three candidates, receiving 9, 5 and then 0 reported
violation entries. Its final edits reduce Nagoya Express from eight cranes to its six-crane limit and move
CMA CGM Montreal from hour 131 to 133. The accepted 17-ship plan incurs **72 in weighted delay cost**, plus
**three berth changes at 5 each**, for a total cost of **87**. CP-SAT’s proven optimum is also 87: the gap is zero,
so `0.2 + 0.8 × exp(0) = 1.00`. That is the recorded submission reward. The animation does not affect the score.
