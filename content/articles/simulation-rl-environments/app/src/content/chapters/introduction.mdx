## Simulation RL environments

Most RL environments for language models are puzzles, code, maths or web tasks. A lot of valuable work looks
different. Someone keeps a schedule running under real rules while things keep going wrong. I want agents that can do
that work, which raises a general question. How do we build simulation environments, digital twins of real work, that
are realistic enough to learn from?

Each part of this series takes one real job, rebuilds it from the records it leaves behind, and grades the agent
against an answer that can be proven best.
