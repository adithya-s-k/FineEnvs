## Simulation RL environments

**The general question: how do we build realistic simulation environments, digital twins of real work, that agents
can learn from?** This series is a general-purpose attempt at an answer, one real job at a time.

Most RL environments for language models are puzzles, code, maths or web tasks. A lot of valuable work looks
different: keeping a schedule under real rules while things keep going wrong. **From seed data we can build realistic
environments, but how close to the real world can we make them?** Each part of this series takes one real job,
rebuilds it from its records, and grades the agent against an answer that can be proven best.
