## PortSimEnv

**Built on real Port of Barcelona data.** PortSimEnv (v1) starts from **1,784 container-ship calls recorded in
2024** at BEST and APM: real ships, dimensions, berth times and assigned quay sections. The agent replans an
actual port week under simulated operational disruptions.

A deterministic RL environment for **berth planning** at the Port of Barcelona. The agent gets one quay, the ships
that really called there in a 2024 week, and a week that has just gone wrong. It re-plans: for every ship, when it
docks, where along the quay, and with how many cranes.

Every plan plays out on a 3D twin of the real port, built from open data: the agent's berthing hours, quay sections
and crane counts become ships, tugs and cranes moving through the week.

<HtmlEmbed src="portsim-gallery.html" wide frameless />

What goes wrong is what goes wrong in real ports: Red Sea diversions sending ships in late and in bunches; gales that
stop ships of 300 m or more above 25 knots and every ship above 30; a crane down for two days; an emergency or a ship
diverted from the other terminal that needs a berth now.
