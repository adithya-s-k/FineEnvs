## The eval

Six models, 50 held-out weeks, one rollout each: the same three tools, 12 turns and 32k output tokens per turn.

<HtmlEmbed
  src="d3-portsim-eval.html"
  title="The eval at a glance"
  desc="Each bar is one model's 50 eval weeks by outcome; the number is its mean reward. Hover for counts."
/>

- **GPT-6.1 Sol leads (0.89)** and submits a valid plan every week, but matches the optimum on only 26 of 50, so
  the benchmark is far from saturated.
- **Every model drops as weeks get harder**: storm and extreme weeks stack gales, closures and emergencies.
- **Open models lose most of their score before grading**: they spend their output budget reasoning and never call
  `submit_plan`. When they do submit a valid plan they average 0.76 to 0.97. Getting them to commit is the first
  thing RL has to fix.
