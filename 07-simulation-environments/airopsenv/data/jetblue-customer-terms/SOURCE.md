# JetBlue's Contract of Carriage and Customer Service Plan (v3)

Nothing committed but the rules: v3 rebooks a disrupted passenger by them in `programs/pax.py`, whose module docstring
quotes the words each rule rests on. No copy of either document is in this repository.

- **Source:**
  - JetBlue Airways, Contract of Carriage, read in the revisions of 2022-08-31 (in force in December 2022), 2025-06-27
    and 2026-07-29 (Rev 88, 98 and 103), whose §25 and §37 read word for word the same; the current one is
    https://cms.jetblue.com/public/dam/ui-assets/p/legal/Contract-of-Carriage--2026.07.29-.pdf, and earlier ones are
    archived by the Internet Archive at
    `https://web.archive.org/web/<timestamp>/https://www.jetblue.com/magnoliapublic/dam/ui-assets/p/contract_of_carriage.pdf`
    (the 2022-08-31 revision in the capture 20221221235240);
  - JetBlue Airways, Customer Service Plan, https://www.jetblue.com/customer-assurance/customer-service-plan, read in its
    archived captures from 2023-05-08 to 2026-08-08 (for example
    https://web.archive.org/web/20250527194658/https://www.jetblue.com/magnoliapublic/.rest/jetblue/v4/page/home/customer-assurance/customer-service-plan/)
    and live on 2026-10-20.
- **Licence:** JetBlue's documents, not public domain. The rules are facts cited from them; no text of theirs is
  redistributed beyond the short quotes that identify a rule.
- **Extraction:**
  - **JetBlue only, the next available flight.** §37(c): on a cancellation, a refund or "reaccommodation on the next
    available JetBlue flight in the same class of service at no additional charge"; §25(a) gives no right to another
    airline. A disrupted passenger is rebooked from the minute of the decision onto JetBlue flights only.
  - **Five days, then a refund.** The Customer Service Plan offers to rebook "to an alternate flight within 5 days" or
    a refund, in its captures from 2025-05-27 on ("to an alternate JetBlue flight within five days" from 2026-07-27).
    A passenger with no JetBlue seat that day takes the first seats of the next five days, and one with none is
    refunded. The same window is applied on every date, though the wording dates from 2025.
  - **Nothing owed on a storm day.** The contract's force majeure covers weather and air traffic control and, by its
    chain rule, the disruptions that follow from them, so the plan's compensation, meals and hotels aren't owed and P
    charges none; refunds and rebooking still apply.
- **What it is and is not:** JetBlue's published terms, not its practice on the day: whether its agents rebooked
  anyone onto another airline, by hand or by choice of the passenger, isn't public, and v3 doesn't model it.
